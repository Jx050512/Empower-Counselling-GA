Privacy Policy

This page explains how visitor information is handled during contact and booking processes.

Booking & Enquiry Forms

When you submit a booking or general enquiry, the basic contact details, selected service and message you provide are stored in protected website storage so Empower can follow up. The information is not displayed publicly and can only be viewed and managed by authorised administrators.

Use and Deletion of Information

Form information is used only to process bookings, respond to enquiries and related administrative follow-up. To request correction or deletion of submitted information, contact Empower through the WhatsApp or phone details listed on this website.

Please Avoid Submitting Sensitive Information

Please do not submit detailed health information, diagnoses, identity numbers, financial information or other highly sensitive information through website forms. Information requiring further discussion should be handled through an appropriate professional communication process.

Third-party Platforms

When you click WhatsApp, Facebook or YouTube links, you leave this website and use the relevant third-party platform. Data handling on those platforms is governed by their own policies.

Website Technical Data

The website hosting platform may process general technical information such as IP address, browser type and request logs for security, performance and basic analytics. This policy should be updated if additional analytics or tracking tools are enabled.
