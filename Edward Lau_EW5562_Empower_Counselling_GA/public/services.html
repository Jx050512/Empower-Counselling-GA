COUNSELLING SERVICES

Start with what matters
most to you.

Different concerns can affect one another. The categories below help you understand the available support; you do not need to decide which “type of problem” you belong to before seeking help.

01 · INDIVIDUAL

Individual Counselling

Suitable for people who want to work through emotions, stress, relationships, self-understanding, life changes or other personal concerns. Counselling starts with the experiences affecting you most, rather than defining the issue by a label alone.

Topics May Include

  • Anxiety, low mood, stress or emotional changes
  • Self-understanding, confidence and inner conflict
  • Relationships and communication
  • Life transitions, loss or adjustment

02 · PARENT & CHILD

Parent–Child & Family Counselling

Supports parents and children in understanding emotions, expectations and communication patterns within the relationship, while exploring approaches that better fit the family.

  • Parent–child communication difficulties
  • Family conflict and boundaries
  • Relationship changes across developmental stages
  • Parenting and communication stress
Ask About This Service →
03 · COUPLE

Couple & Relationship Counselling

Provides couples with a structured conversation space to understand the feelings, needs, communication cycles and expectations behind recurring relationship difficulties.

  • Recurring arguments or reduced communication
  • Trust, emotional distance and relationship needs
  • Relationship stress during life transitions
  • Improving communication and mutual understanding
Ask About This Service →
04 · EDUCATION & CAREER

Education & Career Counselling

Brings together interests, abilities, values, opportunities and real-life considerations to help students or adults make better-informed decisions about their next stage.

  • Education pathways and field of study choices
  • Career interests and career exploration
  • Weighing multiple options
  • Feeling uncertain or anxious about future decisions
Ask About This Service →
05 · MINDFULNESS

Mindfulness & Emotional Regulation

Uses mindfulness-based approaches and related practices to strengthen awareness of present-moment experiences and support healthier responses to anxiety, low mood and stress.

Website content describes services and is not medical diagnosis or an emergency crisis service. If there is immediate danger or urgent medical need, contact local emergency services or an appropriate medical facility.
Ask About This Service →
06 · TRAINING

Counselling Skills Training

Offers training, talks and workshops for groups or participants interested in counselling skills, communication, relationships and personal growth. Topics and audiences are updated according to each programme.
