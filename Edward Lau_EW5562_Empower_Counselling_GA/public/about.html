OFFICIAL BRAND
COUNSELLOR

Counsellor Benson Lim

Benson Lim provides individual, parent–child, couple and education/career counselling to help clients make sense of themselves, their relationships and their direction at different stages of life.

He also provides counselling skills training and incorporates mindfulness-based approaches to support awareness and emotional regulation when facing anxiety, low mood and stress.

COUNSELLING APPROACH

Counselling Approach

Counselling is not about deciding the answer for you. It is a respectful, structured professional relationship where experiences, emotions, needs and choices can be understood together. The aim is not to reduce a complex life situation to a label, but to help you see your next steps more clearly.

“Understanding can be the first step toward finding your own direction.”
AREAS OF FOCUS

Areas of Focus

  • Education choices, career direction and personal development
  • Parent–child relationships, family communication and mutual understanding
  • Couple relationships and emotional communication
  • Emotions, stress, relationships and self-exploration
  • Mindfulness-based support and awareness of anxiety or low mood
  • Counselling skills and communication training
